How it works

Up and running in 3 steps

1

Download SpotiFetch.exe

Click the download button and save the file anywhere on your PC. No installation wizard needed.

2

Double-click to launch

SpotiFetch starts a local server and automatically opens the app in your browser.

3

Paste a link and download

Copy any song or video URL from Spotify, YouTube, TikTok or Instagram and hit Download.
